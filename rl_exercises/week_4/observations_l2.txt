DISCUSSION
----------

What changes when using RLiable vs. plain averages?<br
- RLiable provides Median, IQM, Mean with confidence intervals
- Plain averages only show mean ± std dev
- RLiable's IQM focuses on the middle 50% of results, making it more robust to outliers
- Confidence intervals show reliability of estimates
- Mean (197.90) > Median suggests some high-performing outliers

Do you feel more confident in the results?<br
Yes, we feel more confident in the results. RLiable provides multiple metrics and confidence intervals
and by that gives us a more complete picture of performance variability across seeds.
The range of 78.20 between best and worst seed shows the importance of multi-seed evaluation.
